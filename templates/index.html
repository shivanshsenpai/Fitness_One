Pose Execution & Angle Rules

The HUD frame turns GREEN when pose alignment is 100% perfect, and displays exact degree adjustment commands when aligning.

T-Pose GREEN ON 180°
  1. Stand straight with feet together.
  2. Lift both arms laterally until shoulder-height.
  3. Keep elbows straight at 180° horizontal angle.
Target: Both Elbows 165° – 195°
Tree Pose Vrikshasana
  1. Balance weight firmly on standing leg.
  2. Lift opposite foot and place on inner thigh.
  3. Keep standing knee locked at 180°.
Target: Bent Knee ≤ 90°, Standing Knee ≥ 160°
Warrior Pose Virabhadrasana
  1. Step feet wide apart (~4 ft).
  2. Lunge front leg until knee forms 90° angle.
  3. Extend arms parallel to floor.
Target: Front Knee 85° – 95°
Downward Dog Adho Mukha
  1. Form an inverted V-shape with your body.
  2. Press palms firmly into mat.
  3. Push hips upward & back.
Target: Hip Angle ≥ 160°
Cobra Pose Bhujangasana
  1. Lie prone on stomach with legs extended.
  2. Place palms under shoulders & lift chest.
  3. Keep soft bend in elbows (~45°).
Target: Elbow Arch 35° – 55°
